# 12. Users of the System

## 12.1 User Roles

| Role | Who they are | What they mainly do |
|---|---|---|
| **Guest** | A visitor who is not logged in | Browse and search products; register or log in |
| **Customer** | A registered buyer | Maintain a cart, place and pay for orders, and track them |
| **Seller** | An approved store owner | Manage products, get category suggestions, fulfil order items, view store reports |
| **Admin** | A platform administrator | Approve sellers, oversee all orders, and view management reports |

A person registers either as a customer or as a seller. A seller account is not allowed to list products until an administrator approves it. Administrator accounts are created by the platform owner and cannot be self-registered.

## 12.3 User Characteristics

| Role | Technical skill expected | Typical usage |
|---|---|---|
| Guest and Customer | Basic: comfortable with online shopping | Short, frequent sessions, often on a mobile phone |
| Seller | Basic to moderate: can fill forms and read tables | Regular sessions on desktop or mobile to manage listings and orders |
| Admin | Moderate: understands reports and approval workflows | Occasional sessions on desktop |

## 12.4 How Users Access the System

All users access ShopSphere through a web browser; no installation is needed. Customers and sellers use the same login page and are taken to different screens according to their role (see section 16). Administrators use the same page and are taken to the dashboard.
